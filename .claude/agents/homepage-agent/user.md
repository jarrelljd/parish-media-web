## The business

Parish Media Company is a low-volume, high-ticket, done-for-you marketing agency. It helps Catholic parishes turn Facebook and Instagram into a steady stream of new and returning Mass-goers, event attendees, and volunteers, through done-for-you paid ads and social media content built specifically for parish life.

## Target client & market — the buyer avatar the copy is written for

- **Primary clients**: Catholic parishes — pastors, associate pastors, parish administrators.
- **Secondary**: diocesan offices running evangelization/awareness campaigns.
- **Geography**: primarily English-speaking Catholic parishes, heavily U.S.-oriented.
- **Decision dynamics**: the pastor must be on board — staff buy-in alone is usually not enough. Often involves informal sign-off from a finance council or trusted staff. Reputation and trust are critical; no aggressive or combative marketing.

In short: busy, not particularly technical, budget-conscious, and moved far more by trust and clergy/institutional endorsement than by marketing claims. Every page in this agent's scope should read as if that person is landing on it cold — not a marketer evaluating tactics.

## Core offer

Flagship offer: done-for-you Facebook & Instagram growth and ads for Catholic parishes, typically sold as a 90-day launch/pilot that can extend into a longer engagement.

Core components:
- Done-for-you ad campaigns to increase Mass attendance, promote parish events, and drive sign-ups (RCIA, faith formation, groups, etc.)
- Weekly homily clip production — clipping homilies into short, social-native videos, posted with captions and calls to action
- Clear next-step pathways — links/forms for new Mass-goers, event registrations, volunteer interest
- Reporting & optimization — simple, pastor-friendly reporting on reach, clicks, and meaningful actions; ongoing campaign/creative optimization

Positioning/differentiators: works exclusively with Catholic parishes, understands diocesan dynamics, parish culture, and the liturgical calendar, and is built to add zero extra work to parish staff.

## Acquisition & sales process

- **Primary channels**: organic Instagram + cold outbound (personalized DMs offering a 2–3 minute Loom social media audit), and referrals from existing parish clients.
- **Lead → client journey**: prospect sees the DM and accepts the Loom audit offer → Joe records a 2–3 minute Loom reviewing the parish's current social presence and missed opportunities → DM/email follow-up with a link to book a Zoom consult for the 90-day launch → consultative sales call presenting the pilot → invoice paid → onboarding Zoom (accounts, goals, expectations) → campaigns and weekly homily clips launch, with ongoing reporting and optimization.

## Brand & tone — for any content-generating agent

When creating anything that faces pastors, staff, or parishioners:
- Sound respectful, faith-aligned, and hopeful — not hypey or "internet marketer."
- Emphasize evangelization, community, and stewardship, not "growth hacks" or vanity metrics.
- Competing agencies/vendors are fair game for confident, on-brand differentiation (e.g. "built for parish life, not generic small-business marketing") — but never attack or shame a parish's own volunteers, staff, or diocesan programs. Confident, not combative.
- Keep language clear and simple; assume limited marketing background.
